# Partnership, investment and acquisition

IA NAVA is presenting NAVA to technology partners, industrial users, software publishers, investors and potential acquirers interested in agentic automation on Windows.

## What can be explored

- **Industrial or software partnerships:** validate workflows in controlled environments and connect NAVA to complementary applications.
- **Pilot deployments:** define a limited business mission, acceptance criteria, safeguards and measurable evidence.
- **Investment discussions:** support consolidation of the prototype, evaluation infrastructure and productization work.
- **Acquisition or strategic collaboration:** assess the technology, intellectual property, team, documentation and transition requirements under appropriate confidentiality terms.

## What a serious evaluation should cover

1. A clearly bounded user mission and authorized environment.
2. Reproducible demonstrations using real Windows applications.
3. Verification of produced files, application state and remaining outcomes.
4. Failure, recovery, approval and data-handling behavior.
5. A documented distinction between prototype evidence and production readiness.

NAVA is a proprietary project. Technical details, source code, methods and sensitive evidence are shared selectively and only through an agreed evaluation process.
